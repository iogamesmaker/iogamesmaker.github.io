# Mesopotamia
In the Netherlands some students have to do a finals project before they graduate high school.<br>
For our finals project, a classmate and I made a playable game!<br>
My friend did 99% of the art and historical research, I wrote the code. Raw C++, only external deps are the perlin noise header by Reputeless and SDL2.<br>
If you're interested in playing it, be warned, it's VERY unbalanced. Gameplay is pretty fun once you get the hang of it (imo). It's on the harder side of things starting out, but once you get the gist of it it shouldn't be very difficult. It'll keep you entertained for maybe 30 to 45 minutes.<br>
<br>
The soundtrack is absolutely goated, created by Alexander Ahura for one of my favorite flash games of all times: Snail Bob 3. The composer was fine with us using it, but told me the rights belonged to Hunter Hamster. I mailed them, got no reply, so yeah, snail bob 3 soundtrack!<br>
<small>if you represent hunter hamster studios shoot me an email and i'll remove the music and replace it with some other cool shit, i assume it won't be a huge deal.</small><br>
<br>You can play it in the browser, or download it on the [itch.io page](https://iogamesplayer.itch.io/Mesopotamia).<br><br><br>
